Privacy Policy

Japan Driving Test Master is designed to help users prepare for Japanese driving license examinations through study reviewers, mock exams, and practice questions.

We value your privacy and only collect information necessary to provide app functionality and improve the learning experience.

How Your Information Is Used

  • To improve app stability, features, and user experience
  • To respond to support requests or feedback

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Passwords are stored securely and are not visible to administrators in plain text.
