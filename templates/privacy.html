Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

Japan Driving Test Master ("the App") is designed to help users prepare for Japanese driving license examinations through study reviewers, practice questions, road sign reviews, and mock examinations.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Username and email address provided during account registration
  • Encrypted account credentials required for authentication
  • Practice scores, exam history, and study progress
  • Basic device, browser, and application usage information necessary for security and service operation

How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users and provide secure access to the App
  • Save study progress, scores, and examination history
  • Provide premium subscription features
  • Improve application functionality, stability, and user experience
  • Respond to support requests and user inquiries

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Passwords are securely encrypted and are not accessible in plain text.

Subscriptions

The App may offer premium subscription features. Subscription information may be used to verify premium access and manage subscription benefits.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party hosting, infrastructure, and payment service providers necessary for operation and maintenance.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with advertisers or unrelated third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

User information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide services, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for driving license learners and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the App and on the official website.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us through the App's Contact page or the support email provided within the App.
