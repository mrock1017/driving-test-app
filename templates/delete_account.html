Account Deletion Request

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data from Japan Driving Test Master.

To request account deletion, please contact support through the Contact Us page within the application and provide:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your username (if available)

After verification, your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our systems, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate operational purposes.

Data that may be deleted includes:

  • Account information
  • Study progress
  • Exam history
  • Score history
  • Subscription records associated with the account

For assistance, please use the Contact Us page available within the application.
