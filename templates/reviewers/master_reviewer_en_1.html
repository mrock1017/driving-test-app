🚧 Road Signs & Road Markings

🚫 Do Not Enter & Vehicle Restriction Signs

Restriction signs prohibit certain movements or vehicle types from entering specific roads or lanes.

  • Always check the sign direction carefully.
  • Do not enter prohibited roads or lanes.
  • Some roads restrict motorcycles, mopeds, or small vehicles.
  • Follow all posted lane instructions.
Never assume a road is open just because other vehicles are using it.

🚫 Overtaking Prohibited Zones

Overtaking-prohibited signs and markings completely prohibit passing another vehicle.

  • Overtaking may still be illegal even without crossing the center line.
  • Slow down and remain patient.
  • Watch carefully for curves, pedestrians, and bicycles.
Overtaking restrictions are commonly used in dangerous areas with limited visibility.

🔔 Mandatory Horn Zones

Mandatory horn signs are commonly placed near blind curves and mountain roads with poor visibility.

  • Sound the horn briefly to warn approaching traffic.
  • Use the horn carefully and responsibly.
  • Remain alert for hidden vehicles or pedestrians.

🚲 Bicycle Lanes & Bicycle Crossings

Drivers must understand bicycle-related road markings and respect cyclist priority areas.

  • Do not stop or park on bicycle lanes.
  • Slow down near bicycle crossings.
  • Watch for cyclists approaching from behind before turning.
  • Leave enough space when passing bicycles.
Check Blind Spots Yield to Cyclists Reduce Speed

⚠️ Warning Signs

Warning signs alert drivers to dangerous road conditions ahead.

  • Lane reduction signs warn that lanes decrease ahead.
  • Falling rock signs appear in mountainous areas.
  • Construction signs require slower and more careful driving.
  • Speed limit signs show the legal maximum speed.
Reduce speed early when approaching warning signs.

🚦 Intersections, Signals & Turning Rules

🟢 Green Lights & Safe Entry

A green signal does not always mean it is safe to enter the intersection.

  • Do not enter if traffic congestion may trap your vehicle.
  • Never block crosswalks or railroad crossings.
  • Confirm there is enough space ahead before moving.
Blocking intersections creates danger for all road users.

➡️ Arrow Signals & Special Turn Permissions

Arrow signals only permit movement in the directions shown.

  • Wait for the correct arrow before turning.
  • Some intersections allow turns during red or yellow signals with special signs.
  • Always check pedestrians and bicycles first.
Pedestrian safety always has priority during turns.

🔴 Flashing Red Signals & Stop Procedures

Flashing red signals require a complete stop before proceeding safely.

  • Treat flashing red lights like stop signs.
  • Stop where visibility is safest if no stop line exists.
  • Check both directions carefully before moving.

🚗 Turning & Lane Change Safety

Safe turning and lane changing require proper observation and signaling.

  • Check mirrors and blind spots before moving.
  • Signal before changing lanes or turning.
  • Keep signaling until the maneuver is complete.
  • Rear wheels follow a tighter path during turns.
Turning too sharply may cause wheels to hit curbs or pedestrians.

🚓 Police Officer Instructions

Police officer directions take priority over traffic lights and signs.

  • Follow hand signals immediately.
  • Stay calm during congestion or emergencies.
  • Watch surrounding traffic carefully.

🚗 Priority Roads

Vehicles on main roads generally have priority over vehicles entering from side roads.

  • Reduce speed before entering intersections.
  • Yield carefully when visibility is limited.
  • Watch for hidden motorcycles and bicycles.

🚶 Pedestrians, Cyclists & School Zones

🚸 Children & School Areas

Areas near schools, parks, and residential zones require extra caution.

  • Children may suddenly run into the road.
  • Reduce speed and prepare to stop.
  • Watch carefully between parked vehicles.
Hidden child hazards are common in residential neighborhoods.

🚲 Bicycle Safety

Cyclists are vulnerable road users and may move unpredictably.

  • Leave enough side distance when passing.
  • Do not force turns ahead of bicycles.
  • Watch carefully for cyclists approaching from behind.
Cyclists may suddenly avoid obstacles or wobble unexpectedly.

🚶 Sidewalks & Crossing Sidewalk Areas

Drivers entering or leaving parking areas must carefully check sidewalks.

  • Stop before crossing sidewalks if necessary.
  • Check for pedestrians and bicycles.
  • Never place vehicle tires onto sidewalks unless permitted.

🚸 Safety Zones

Safety zones protect pedestrians and organize traffic flow.

  • Slow down near safety zones.
  • Do not park, stop, or block these areas.
  • Watch carefully for pedestrians crossing nearby.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚫 No Parking & No Stopping Areas

Japanese traffic law strictly regulates where drivers may park or stop.

  • Do not park near crosswalks or bicycle crossings.
  • Parking near safety zones is prohibited.
  • Garage entrances must remain clear.
  • Waiting for someone may still count as parking.
Illegal parking may block visibility and increase accident risk.

🚫 Double Parking

Stopping beside another parked vehicle is generally prohibited.

  • Blocks traffic flow.
  • Creates danger for cyclists.
  • Reduces visibility for surrounding traffic.

🚌 Bus Stops & Public Areas

Parking restrictions often apply near bus stops and public transportation areas.

  • Always obey posted signs and markings.
  • Do not block buses or passengers.
  • Check operating conditions carefully.

🅿️ Proper Parking Responsibility

Vehicle owners should secure legal off-road parking spaces.

  • Helps reduce roadside congestion.
  • Improves neighborhood safety.
  • Supports smoother traffic flow.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety & Riding Techniques

🪖 Protective Equipment

Motorcycle riders should wear proper protective equipment at all times.

  • Use certified helmets with PS(C) or JIS marks.
  • Wear long sleeves, gloves, and protective clothing.
  • Protect exposed skin even during summer.

🧍 Proper Riding Position

Stable posture improves motorcycle balance and control.

  • Grip the fuel tank with both knees.
  • Keep toes pointed inward or forward.
  • Hold handlebars gently but firmly.
  • Reduce unnecessary body movement.

🛑 Braking Techniques

Riders should use both front and rear brakes together smoothly.

  • Keep the motorcycle balanced while braking.
  • Avoid sudden braking on slippery roads.
  • Brake gently to prevent skidding.
Balanced braking improves stopping control and stability.

👥 Passenger & Cargo Rules

Passengers and cargo must be carried safely and legally.

  • Passengers are only allowed on suitable motorcycles.
  • Ordinary mopeds cannot carry passengers.
  • Secure cargo properly.
  • Cargo should not extend excessively from the sides.

↪️ Two-Stage Right Turns

Some motorized bicycles must perform two-stage right turns at large intersections.

  • Follow road markings carefully.
  • Obey intersection signs and signals.
  • Move carefully through each stage.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

🔧 Vehicle Inspection Before Driving

Before entering the expressway, inspect your vehicle carefully.

  • Check fuel level.
  • Inspect tires, brakes, and lights.
  • Check engine oil and coolant.
  • Confirm washer fluid levels.

🚘 Safe Merging & Exiting

Use acceleration and deceleration lanes correctly.

  • Match traffic speed before merging.
  • Check mirrors and blind spots carefully.
  • Slow down only after entering the deceleration lane.
Slowing down too early in the main lane may cause rear-end collisions.

🛑 Stopping on Expressways

Stopping or parking on expressways is generally prohibited except during emergencies.

  • Breakdowns
  • Traffic accidents
  • Police instructions

📻 Traffic & Weather Information

Gather traffic and weather information before expressway driving.

  • Check radio traffic reports.
  • Avoid dangerous weather conditions.
  • Plan rest stops during long trips.

🚫 Unsafe Towing

Towing with ropes or cables on expressways is generally prohibited because it is dangerous at high speeds.

🌧️ Weather, Curves & Night Driving

🌧️ Rainy Weather & Hydroplaning

Wet roads reduce tire grip and increase stopping distance.

  • Reduce speed during rain.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Brake gently and early.
  • Avoid sudden steering movements.
Roads are especially slippery when rain first begins.

❄️ Snow & Ice Safety

Snowy and icy roads are extremely dangerous and require special caution.

  • Use winter tires or tire chains.
  • Avoid sudden braking.
  • Accelerate and steer gently.

🌙 Night Driving

Visibility becomes weaker at night, increasing accident risk.

  • Reduce speed at night.
  • Use low beams in busy areas.
  • Avoid staring directly into headlights.
  • Watch carefully for pedestrians and bicycles.
Eyes need time to adjust when moving from bright areas into darkness.

🌪️ Curves & Centrifugal Force

Higher speeds and sharper curves increase outward force during turns.

  • Reduce speed before entering curves.
  • Avoid sudden steering.
  • Maintain stable vehicle control.

🚄 Railroad Crossing Safety

🚉 Safe Railroad Crossing Procedures

Railroad crossings require careful observation and caution.

  • Never stop on railroad tracks.
  • Check signals, barriers, and warning lights.
  • Be prepared to stop immediately if danger appears.
  • Signal-controlled crossings may be crossed according to signal indications.

🚗 Vehicle Stuck on Tracks

If your vehicle becomes stuck on railroad tracks:

  • Warn railway personnel immediately.
  • Move the vehicle if possible.
  • Escape safely if necessary.
Never remain inside a vehicle if a train is approaching.

🚑 Emergency Situations & Legal Responsibility

🚨 Emergency Vehicles

Drivers must safely yield when emergency vehicles approach.

  • Move safely out of the way.
  • Do not block intersections.
  • On one-way roads, either side may be used if safer.

🌏 Earthquakes & Disasters

During earthquakes or major disasters:

  • Avoid sudden braking.
  • Stop safely after checking surroundings.
  • Keep emergency routes clear.
  • Do not block emergency responders.

🩹 Accidents & First Aid

Drivers must respond responsibly after accidents.

  • Help injured people carefully.
  • Avoid moving people with neck injuries unless necessary.
  • Call police and emergency services immediately.
  • Even minor accidents must be reported to police.
Official police reports are necessary for legal and insurance procedures.

😴 Fatigue & Safe Driving Mindset

Safe driving depends on patience, awareness, and physical condition.

  • Do not drive while tired.
  • Maintain safe following distance.
  • Drive cooperatively and respectfully.
  • Avoid reckless or selfish behavior.
Fatigue greatly reduces reaction time and concentration.

⚙️ Engine Braking & Downhill Safety

Engine braking helps control vehicle speed safely on downhill roads.

  • Lower gears provide stronger engine braking.
  • Reduces brake overheating.
  • Helps maintain safer speed control.

✅ Key Takeaways

🚦 Intersections & Signals

  • Green lights do not guarantee safety.
  • Always check pedestrians and bicycles before turning.
  • Follow police officer instructions immediately.

🚶 Pedestrian & Bicycle Safety

  • Reduce speed near schools and residential areas.
  • Leave enough space when passing cyclists.
  • Never block crosswalks or bicycle crossings.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety

  • Wear certified helmets and protective gear.
  • Use both brakes smoothly.
  • Maintain stable riding posture.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

  • Inspect your vehicle before long trips.
  • Use acceleration and deceleration lanes properly.
  • Stop only during emergencies.

🌧️ Weather & Night Driving

  • Reduce speed during rain, snow, and darkness.
  • Avoid sudden steering and braking.
  • Increase following distance on slippery roads.

🚑 Emergency Preparedness

  • Yield quickly to emergency vehicles.
  • Report accidents to police.
  • Stay calm during emergencies and disasters.
