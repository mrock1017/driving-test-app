🚘 General Driving & Vehicle Control

⚡ Speed, Braking Distance & Centrifugal Force

Higher speed greatly increases stopping distance and accident severity.

  • Fast driving reduces reaction time.
  • Curves become more dangerous at higher speeds.
  • Centrifugal force becomes stronger during turns.
  • Safe speed control is essential for accident prevention.
Excessive speed narrows your field of vision and makes hazards harder to detect.

🌀 Safe Curve & Turning Techniques

Drivers should reduce speed before entering curves or making turns.

  • Brake before the curve, not during it.
  • Accelerate gradually after exiting.
  • Maintain stable steering control.
  • Avoid sudden steering or braking.
Slow Before Curves Smooth Steering Stable Control

⛰️ Downhill Driving & Engine Braking

Lower gears provide stronger engine braking and help control vehicle speed safely downhill.

  • Use lower gears like “2,” “1,” or “L” on steep slopes.
  • Reduces brake overheating.
  • Improves downhill stability.
  • Helps prevent brake failure.

🧍 Proper Driving Posture

Good driving posture improves control, comfort, and emergency response ability.

  • Keep both hands ready on the steering wheel.
  • Sit upright with relaxed shoulders.
  • Keep knees slightly bent.
  • Adjust mirrors and seating properly.

😴 Fatigue & Long-Distance Driving

Driver fatigue reduces concentration, steering ability, and reaction time.

  • Take breaks approximately every 2 hours.
  • Do not continue driving while sleepy.
  • Fatigue increases accident risk significantly.
Long periods of high-speed driving may distort a driver’s sense of speed.

👀 Wide Observation & Hazard Awareness

Safe drivers observe the road broadly instead of focusing on only one point.

  • Watch intersections, mirrors, and sidewalks.
  • Detect hazards earlier.
  • Stay aware of changing traffic conditions.
  • Never assume other drivers will stop.

🚦 Intersections & Right-of-Way Rules

🛑 Unsignalized & Uncontrolled Intersections

Drivers must slow down and confirm safety carefully even when intersections appear clear.

  • Vehicles from the right often have priority.
  • Wider roads generally have priority over narrower roads.
  • Check carefully for hidden vehicles, pedestrians, and bicycles.
Never assume another vehicle will stop automatically.

🚦 Green Lights & Green Arrow Signals

A green light does not guarantee it is safe to proceed.

  • Do not enter intersections if congestion may trap your vehicle.
  • Arrow signals allow movement only in the shown direction.
  • Always check pedestrians and bicycles first.
Directional arrows may allow movement even if the main signal is red.

👮 Police Officer Instructions

Police officer hand signals and traffic controllers take priority over traffic lights and signs.

  • Obey directions immediately.
  • Watch carefully during congestion or emergencies.
  • Stay calm and cooperative.

👀 Blind Spots While Turning

Blind spots become especially dangerous during right and left turns.

  • Motorcycles may be hidden behind vehicles.
  • Pedestrians may suddenly appear.
  • Always check mirrors and blind spots carefully.

🚘 Merging & Lane Changes

Vehicles entering traffic must merge smoothly and safely.

  • Main-lane traffic generally has priority.
  • Signal before changing lanes.
  • Check mirrors and blind spots carefully.
  • Do not force entry into traffic.

🏍️ Two-Stage Right Turns

Ordinary motorized bicycles usually perform two-stage right turns at large intersections.

  • Cross straight first.
  • Stop safely near the center.
  • Complete the turn when permitted.

🚧 Road Signs & Traffic Signs

🚫 Restriction & Prohibition Signs

Restriction signs prohibit certain movements, vehicles, or actions.

  • Do not enter prohibited roads or lanes.
  • Some roads prohibit motorcycles or small vehicles.
  • No stopping signs prohibit even temporary stopping.
  • Always follow lane restrictions carefully.

↔️ Mandatory Direction Signs

Blue directional signs instruct drivers to move only in the indicated direction.

  • These are instructions, not suggestions.
  • Follow arrows and lane markings carefully.
  • Check surrounding traffic before moving.

🔔 Sound Horn Signs

Sound horn signs appear in low-visibility areas such as blind curves and mountain roads.

  • Warns approaching traffic.
  • Use the horn briefly and responsibly.
  • Avoid unnecessary horn use.

🏫 School Zones & Pedestrian Areas

School zones and pedestrian-only roads require extra caution.

  • Reduce speed immediately.
  • Watch carefully for children.
  • Pedestrian safety always has priority.
Children may suddenly enter the roadway without warning.

🚧 Warning Signs

Warning signs alert drivers to dangerous road conditions ahead.

  • Lane reduction signs warn that lanes will decrease.
  • Strong wind signs warn about dangerous crosswinds.
  • Falling rock signs appear in mountainous areas.

🚶 Pedestrians & Bicycle Safety

🚸 Pedestrians Near Intersections

Drivers must protect pedestrians even when no marked crosswalk exists.

  • Reduce speed near intersections.
  • Prepare to stop at any time.
  • Never interfere with pedestrian movement.

🚲 Bicycle Safety & Passing Distance

Cyclists are vulnerable road users and may move unpredictably.

  • Leave enough space when passing bicycles.
  • Reduce speed near bicycle crossings.
  • Watch for hidden cyclists near stopped vehicles.
Cyclists may suddenly avoid obstacles or wobble unexpectedly.

🚋 Streetcars, Bus Stops & Public Transport Areas

Drivers should use extra caution near public transportation areas.

  • Passengers may suddenly enter the roadway.
  • Slow down near safety zones.
  • Do not block buses or passengers.

🧍 Sidewalks & Pedestrian Areas

Sidewalks must remain safe and accessible for pedestrians.

  • Do not block sidewalks.
  • Avoid placing tires onto pedestrian paths.
  • Check carefully when entering or leaving parking areas.

🏍️ Pushing a Motorcycle

A person pushing a motorcycle with the engine off is generally treated similarly to a pedestrian.

  • Sidewalk use may be allowed when done safely.
  • Do not obstruct pedestrians.
  • Move carefully in crowded areas.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety & Riding Techniques

🛡️ Protective Equipment

Motorcycle riders should wear approved helmets and protective clothing at all times.

  • Use PS(C) or JIS approved helmets.
  • Wear gloves and protective clothing.
  • Protect exposed skin even during summer.

🧍 Proper Riding Posture

Stable posture improves motorcycle balance and control.

  • Grip the fuel tank with both knees.
  • Hold handlebars lightly but firmly.
  • Keep wrists angled naturally downward.
  • Reduce unnecessary body movement.

🛑 Motorcycle Braking & Throttle Control

Riders should brake smoothly while maintaining balance and stable posture.

  • Use both front and rear brakes together.
  • Use engine braking when appropriate.
  • Avoid abrupt braking during curves.
  • Accelerate gently after turning.

👥 Passenger Restrictions

Passengers may only ride on motorcycles designed for passenger transport.

  • Ordinary mopeds cannot carry passengers.
  • New riders have additional restrictions.
  • Passengers affect balance and braking performance.

⚠️ Motorcycle Visibility & Blind Spots

Motorcycles are smaller and harder for automobile drivers to notice.

  • Avoid staying beside larger vehicles.
  • Stay visible whenever possible.
  • Never assume drivers can always see you.

🚦 Motorized Bicycle Rules

Ordinary motorized bicycles follow special rules in Japan.

  • Usually limited to 30 km/h.
  • Two-stage right turns may be required.
  • Always follow posted restrictions carefully.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚫 Parking Near Crossings & Safety Zones

Parking or stopping near crosswalks, bicycle crossings, and safety zones is prohibited.

  • Crossing visibility must remain clear.
  • Restrictions often apply within several meters.
  • Hidden pedestrians create serious danger.

🅿️ Parking Near Entrances & Fire Hydrants

Vehicles must not block entrances or emergency equipment.

  • Parking within 3 meters of entrances is prohibited.
  • Do not block garages or parking access points.
  • Fire hydrants must remain accessible.

🚫 Illegal Parking & Waiting

Waiting for someone may still count as illegal parking.

  • Applies even if the driver remains inside.
  • Always obey posted restrictions.
  • Illegal parking notices must be handled properly.

🚗 Proper Parking Procedures

Vehicles should park according to road direction and local rules.

  • Use the parking brake properly.
  • Manual vehicles should not rely only on gear position.
  • Unsafe parking increases accident risk.

🌧️ Weather & Night Driving

🌙 Night Driving Safety

Visibility becomes shorter at night, making hazards harder to notice.

  • Reduce speed at night.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Watch carefully for pedestrians and bicycles.

💡 Headlight Usage & Glare

Proper headlight usage improves visibility and prevents glare for other drivers.

  • Use low beams when following vehicles closely.
  • Avoid staring into oncoming headlights.
  • Looking slightly left may reduce glare.

💦 Rain & Hydroplaning

Rain greatly reduces tire grip and increases stopping distance.

  • Hydroplaning weakens steering and braking control.
  • Reduce speed during rain.
  • Avoid sudden steering or braking.
Roads become especially slippery when rain first begins.

❄️ Snow, Ice & Crosswinds

Snowy roads and strong winds require extra caution.

  • Use winter tires or tire chains.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Keep both hands firmly on the wheel during strong winds.
  • Avoid sudden steering corrections.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

🚘 Safe Expressway Driving

Expressway driving requires higher awareness because vehicles travel at high speeds.

  • Maintain enough following distance.
  • Avoid abrupt braking or steering.
  • Drive smoothly and predictably.

🚗 Lane Usage & Overtaking

Multi-lane roads use different lanes according to traffic speed.

  • Slower vehicles should keep left.
  • Right lanes are mainly for overtaking.
  • Follow lane discipline carefully.

🛠️ Disabled Vehicles & Emergency Stops

Disabled vehicles should move safely to the shoulder whenever possible.

  • Use hazard lights immediately.
  • Avoid blocking traffic lanes.
  • Place warning devices properly.

🚚 Towing Rules

Expressway towing requires proper equipment and safe procedures.

  • Unsafe towing is prohibited.
  • Both vehicles must meet safety standards.
  • Improper towing creates serious accident risk.

🚄 Railroad Crossing Safety

🛑 Railroad Crossing Procedures

Most railroad crossings require careful confirmation and complete stops.

  • Check signals, barriers, and warning lights.
  • Never rush through crossings.
  • Cross only when the road ahead is clear.

🚫 Never Block Railroad Tracks

Drivers must never enter crossings if congestion may trap the vehicle on the tracks.

  • Confirm traffic flow before entering.
  • Trains cannot stop quickly.
  • Blocking tracks creates extreme danger.
Even a few seconds of poor judgment at railroad crossings can cause fatal accidents.

🚑 Emergency Situations & Legal Responsibility

🚨 Emergency Vehicles

Drivers must yield properly when emergency vehicles approach.

  • Clear intersections safely first if already inside.
  • Move aside calmly and carefully.
  • Do not block emergency routes.

🚧 After Traffic Accidents

Drivers must respond responsibly after accidents.

  • Stop the engine if possible.
  • Move vehicles safely when appropriate.
  • Prevent secondary accidents.
  • Report accidents to police.

🛠️ Brake Failure & Sudden Emergencies

Sudden braking should only be used during emergencies or unavoidable danger.

  • Use escape areas or barriers if brakes fail downhill.
  • Reduce impact safely whenever possible.
  • Brake problems require immediate inspection.

🚨 Emergency Equipment

Drivers should prepare emergency equipment before accidents occur.

  • Warning flares
  • Emergency triangles
  • Flashlights and safety tools

🔧 Vehicle Inspection & Maintenance

🛑 Brake Inspection

Brake problems should be inspected immediately.

  • A sinking brake pedal may indicate hydraulic failure.
  • Brake failure creates serious danger.
  • Never ignore warning signs.

🛞 Tire Inspection

Tire condition greatly affects braking and steering safety.

  • Check tread wear indicators regularly.
  • Worn tires reduce traction.
  • Rain and snow increase danger further.

📋 Legal Vehicle Responsibilities

Drivers must follow Japanese vehicle management and licensing regulations.

  • Display required parking registration stickers properly.
  • Follow legal inspection schedules.
  • Learner drivers require proper supervision.

🚘 Overtaking & Passing Rules

🚧 Safe Overtaking Procedures

Overtaking requires careful judgment and proper observation.

  • Check mirrors and blind spots first.
  • Confirm enough passing space exists.
  • Signal only after confirming safety.
  • Do not force dangerous overtaking maneuvers.

🚫 Overtaking Restrictions

Overtaking is prohibited in many dangerous areas.

  • Follow lane markings and road signs carefully.
  • Passing on the left is usually prohibited.
  • Tunnel overtaking depends on lane conditions and signs.

  • Avoid vehicle blind spots
