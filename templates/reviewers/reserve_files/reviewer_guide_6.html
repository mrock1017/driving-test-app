🚦 Intersections & Right-of-Way Rules

🛣️ Wider Road Priority

At intersections without traffic signals, vehicles traveling on wider roads usually have priority.

  • Drivers from narrower roads must slow down.
  • Check traffic carefully before entering.
  • Avoid interfering with vehicles already on the wider road.
Wider roads often carry heavier and faster traffic, so careful entry improves safety.

🚘 Green Arrow Signals

Directional arrow signals allow movement in the shown direction even if the main signal is red or yellow.

  • Always check for pedestrians first.
  • Proceed only when safe.
  • Follow the indicated arrow direction carefully.

🏍️ Two-Stage Right Turns

Ordinary motorized bicycles usually perform two-stage right turns at larger intersections.

  • Cross straight first.
  • Stop safely near the center.
  • Complete the right turn when permitted.
This system reduces collisions with larger vehicles at busy intersections.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

🚚 Expressway Towing Rules

Only properly equipped vehicles may tow on Japanese expressways.

  • The towing vehicle must meet safety standards.
  • The towed vehicle must also be properly designed.
  • Unsafe towing is prohibited.
High-speed towing without proper equipment creates serious accident risks.

🚘 What Counts as an Expressway

The term “expressway” includes national expressways and other high-speed motorways.

  • Special traffic rules apply.
  • Higher speed limits require extra caution.
  • Drivers must maintain safe following distance.

🚗 Multi-Lane Road Usage

On roads with three lanes in the same direction, traffic generally becomes faster from left to right.

  • Slower traffic should keep left.
  • Right lanes are mainly for overtaking.
  • Use lane discipline properly.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety & Riding Techniques

🛑 Proper Motorcycle Braking

Riders should use engine braking together with both front and rear brakes while keeping the motorcycle upright.

  • Keep the front wheel straight.
  • Brake smoothly and evenly.
  • Maintain stable posture while slowing down.
Balanced braking helps prevent skidding and improves motorcycle control.

🌀 Smooth Throttle Control

Gentle throttle control during curves helps maintain motorcycle stability.

  • Avoid abrupt braking in curves.
  • Accelerate smoothly.
  • Maintain balanced cornering posture.

🚗 Motorcycle Blind Spots

Motorcycles are smaller and harder for automobile drivers to notice.

  • Stay alert around larger vehicles.
  • Avoid remaining in blind spots.
  • Never assume drivers can always see you.
Blind spot accidents involving motorcycles often result in severe injuries.

🌧️ Weather & Seasonal Driving

❄️ Snow & Ice Driving

Snowy and icy roads require proper winter equipment and slower driving speeds.

  • Use tire chains or winter tires.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Avoid sudden steering or braking.
Snow and ice greatly reduce tire traction and increase stopping distance.

🚧 Road Signs & Traffic Signs

↩️ Directional Arrow Signs

Blue directional arrow signs beside traffic signals allow movement in the indicated direction.

  • Proceed only in the shown direction.
  • Yield to pedestrians when necessary.
  • Check surroundings carefully before moving.

🚫 Right Turn Restriction Signs

Some road signs prohibit right turns at specific locations.

  • Follow restrictions carefully.
  • Do not ignore signs for convenience.
  • Watch for lane markings and instructions.
Ignoring turn restrictions may create dangerous conflicts with traffic and pedestrians.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🅿️ Parking Near Entrances

Parking is prohibited within 3 meters of parking lot entrances, garages, and vehicle access points.

  • Maintains visibility for drivers.
  • Prevents traffic obstruction.
  • Allows safer entry and exit.

🚓 Illegal Parking Notices

Drivers may remove illegal parking notices themselves after handling the violation properly.

  • No need to wait for police removal.
  • Follow legal procedures carefully.
  • Always park in legal areas afterward.

🚘 Overtaking & Passing Rules

🚧 Overtaking in Tunnels

Overtaking inside tunnels may be allowed if multiple lanes travel in the same direction.

  • Follow lane markings carefully.
  • Confirm safety before passing.
  • Obey tunnel traffic signs.

🚗 Proper Overtaking Procedure

Before overtaking another vehicle, drivers should check traffic conditions first before signaling.

  • Check ahead and behind carefully.
  • Confirm enough passing space exists.
  • Signal only after confirming safety.
Safe overtaking requires careful observation and good judgment.

🚘 Safe Driving Habits

⚠️ Excessive Speed Dangers

Excessive speed reduces reaction time and makes hazards harder to recognize.

  • High speed narrows field of vision.
  • Stopping distance becomes longer.
  • Accidents become more severe.

👀 Wide Field of Vision

Drivers should observe the road broadly instead of focusing on one point.

  • Detect hazards earlier.
  • Watch pedestrians and bicycles.
  • Notice changing traffic conditions quickly.

🔔 Proper Horn Usage

Horns may be used when necessary to prevent immediate danger.

  • Avoid unnecessary horn use.
  • Warn others during emergencies.
  • Use responsibly and carefully.

🔧 Vehicle Inspection & Maintenance

🛑 Brake Problems

A brake pedal sinking fully to the floor may indicate serious brake system failure.

  • Do not continue driving normally.
  • Inspect the vehicle immediately.
  • Brake failure can cause severe accidents.

🛞 Tire Tread Wear Indicators

Tire tread wear indicators help drivers judge whether tires remain safe for use.

  • Check tire condition regularly.
  • Worn tires reduce traction.
  • Rainy weather increases danger.
Proper tire maintenance improves braking and steering safety.

⚖️ Traffic Rules & Legal Responsibility

🚧 Expressway Safety Rules

Expressway driving requires stricter safety awareness because vehicles travel at much higher speeds.

  • Maintain safe following distance.
  • Follow lane rules carefully.
  • Avoid unnecessary stopping.

🚓 Following Road Signs Properly

Traffic signs and lane markings must always be obeyed.

  • Signs override personal convenience.
  • Ignoring restrictions may cause accidents.
  • Violations may lead to penalties.
