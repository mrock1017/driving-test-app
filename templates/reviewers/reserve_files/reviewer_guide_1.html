🚧 Road Signs & Road Markings

🚫 Do Not Enter Sign

This sign prohibits entry from the direction facing the sign. Vehicles may still legally enter from another permitted direction.

  • Always check the sign direction carefully.
  • Do not assume the road is fully closed.

↩️ Special Turn Permission Signs

Some intersections allow turns even during a red or yellow signal.

  • Check pedestrians first.
  • Yield to surrounding traffic.
  • Proceed carefully and slowly.
Safety is always more important than signal priority.

🪨 Falling Rocks Warning Sign

This warning sign appears in mountainous or unstable areas where rocks may fall onto the road.

  • Reduce speed.
  • Stay alert.
  • Avoid sudden steering.

🚫 Overtaking Prohibited Sign

This sign means overtaking is completely prohibited, even without crossing the center line.

🚦 Intersections & Turning Safety

🚴 Watch for Bicycles When Turning

Before turning left, carefully check for bicycles approaching from behind.

  • Never force your turn ahead of a cyclist.
  • Slow down and wait if necessary.
  • The horn does not give you priority.

🟢 Green Light Does Not Always Mean Go

Even with a green signal, drivers must avoid entering intersections if congestion may trap the vehicle.

  • Do not block crosswalks.
  • Do not stop on railroad crossings.
  • Always confirm there is enough space ahead.

➡️ Proper Right Turn Position

Drivers should position the vehicle according to lane markings and road conditions.

Moving too close to the center line unnecessarily may create danger.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚲 Bicycle Crossing Areas

Parking or stopping within 5 meters of a bicycle crossing is prohibited.

🛑 Safety Zones

Both parking and temporary stopping are prohibited near safety zones.

🏠 Garage Entrances

Parking near garage entrances is prohibited, even for the owner.

🚶 Sidewalk Safety

Do not place vehicle tires onto sidewalks unless specifically permitted.

⏱️ Waiting Can Still Be Parking

Remaining stopped in a no-parking area while waiting for someone may still count as illegal parking.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety

🪖 Protective Equipment

  • Wear certified helmets with PS(C) or JIS marks.
  • Use long sleeves and protective clothing.
  • Protect exposed skin even during summer.

🧍 Proper Riding Posture

  • Keep toes pointed inward.
  • Grip handlebars gently but firmly.
  • Keep wrists slightly downward.
Proper posture improves motorcycle balance and control.

👥 Passenger Restrictions

Inexperienced riders are not allowed to carry passengers immediately after obtaining a motorcycle license.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

🔧 Vehicle Inspection Before Driving

  • Check fuel level.
  • Inspect engine oil and coolant.
  • Check tires and lights.

📻 Check Traffic Information

Before entering the expressway, gather traffic and weather information.

  • Use radio broadcasts.
  • Check traffic reports.
  • Avoid dangerous conditions.

🚫 Unsafe Towing

Towing a vehicle with a rope or cable on expressways is generally prohibited because it is dangerous at high speeds.

🚑 Emergency Situations

🚨 Emergency Vehicles

  • Move safely out of the way.
  • Do not block intersections.
  • On one-way roads, either side may be used if safer.

🚄 Vehicle Stuck on Railroad Tracks

  • Warn railway personnel immediately.
  • Move the vehicle if possible.
  • Escape safely if necessary.

🛑 Brake Failure

If brakes fail downhill, using roadside gravel or barriers may help reduce impact severity and save lives.

🛡️ Safety Driving Mindset

🤝 Considerate Driving

  • Respect pedestrians and cyclists.
  • Drive cooperatively with others.
  • Avoid reckless behavior.

🌙 Night Driving Safety

  • Reduce speed at night.
  • Turn headlights on early.
  • Watch carefully for pedestrians and bicycles.

💥 Collision Force Awareness

A collision at 60 km/h can produce impact force similar to falling from a five-story building.

Speed control and careful driving save lives.

✅ Key Takeaways Checklist

🚗 Before Driving

  • Check brakes and lights
  • Inspect fuel and oil
  • Adjust seat position

🚦 During Driving

  • Check blind spots
  • Watch for bicycles
  • Follow road signs

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety

  • Wear certified helmets
  • Use protective clothing
  • Maintain proper posture

🚑 Emergencies

  • Yield to emergency vehicles
  • Act quickly on railroad tracks
  • Stay calm during breakdowns
