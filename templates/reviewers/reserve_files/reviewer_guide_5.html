🚘 General Driving & Vehicle Control

⚡ Speed, Braking Distance & Centrifugal Force

When vehicle speed increases, braking distance and centrifugal force increase dramatically.

  • Doubling speed greatly increases stopping distance.
  • Fast driving makes curves more dangerous.
  • Higher speed reduces emergency reaction time.
Safe speed control is one of the most important driving skills in Japan.

⛰️ Driving Down Steep Slopes

Automatic vehicles should use engine braking by selecting lower gears like “2,” “1,” or “L.”

  • Prevents brake overheating.
  • Improves downhill control.
  • Reduces brake failure risk.

🌀 Curves & Vehicle Stability

Drivers should reduce speed before entering curves regardless of road visibility.

  • Curves increase skidding risk.
  • Centrifugal force becomes stronger in sharp turns.
  • Stable steering improves safety.

↩️ Safe Turning Techniques

Drivers should slow down before left or right turns and accelerate gradually afterward.

  • Improves tire grip.
  • Protects pedestrians and cyclists.
  • Maintains vehicle balance.

🛣️ Long-Distance Driving

Long trips require regular breaks to reduce fatigue and maintain concentration.

  • Rest approximately every 2 hours.
  • Fatigue reduces reaction speed.
  • Tired driving increases accident risk.

🌧️ Weather & Night Driving

🌙 Night Driving Safety

Visibility becomes shorter at night, making pedestrians and bicycles harder to notice.

  • Reduce speed at night.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Watch carefully for dark clothing.

💦 Hydroplaning

Hydroplaning happens when water builds between the tires and road surface.

  • Steering control becomes weaker.
  • Braking ability decreases.
  • High speeds increase danger.
Reduce speed during rain and avoid sudden steering or braking.

💡 Headlight Glare

Bright headlights from oncoming vehicles can temporarily reduce visibility.

  • Look slightly toward the left side.
  • Avoid staring directly at lights.
  • Maintain awareness of lane position.

🔦 Flashing Headlights

Drivers may briefly flash headlights near blind curves or intersections at night.

  • Warns approaching vehicles.
  • Improves visibility awareness.
  • Should not be overused.

🚦 Intersections & Right-of-Way

🚗 Uncontrolled Intersections

At intersections without signals or signs, the vehicle approaching from the right usually has priority.

  • Slow down before entering.
  • Check carefully in all directions.
  • Yield when necessary.

👀 Blind Spots While Turning

Oncoming vehicles may block visibility while waiting to turn right.

  • Motorcycles may be hidden.
  • Pedestrians may suddenly appear.
  • Blind areas grow larger as vehicles approach.

🚫 Never Assume Others Will Stop

Drivers should always prepare for unexpected actions from other road users.

Assuming another vehicle will stop may lead to serious collisions.

👮 Police Officer Signals

Police officer hand directions have the same authority as traffic lights.

  • Drivers must obey immediately.
  • Police directions override traffic signals.
  • Watch carefully during traffic control.

🚦 Green Arrow Signals

Green arrows allow vehicles to move in the shown direction even if the main signal remains red.

  • Passenger cars may proceed.
  • Motorcycles may also proceed unless restricted.
  • Always confirm surrounding safety first.

🚧 Road Signs & Traffic Signs

🏍️ Motorcycle Right Turn Signs

Some signs require ordinary motorized bicycles to make automobile-style right turns.

  • Move toward the roadway center first.
  • Follow lane markings carefully.
  • Obey intersection instructions.

🚫 No Stopping or Parking Zones

Certain signs prohibit both parking and temporary stopping.

  • Do not wait briefly in restricted zones.
  • Keep traffic flowing safely.
  • Always check posted signs carefully.

🚧 Lane Reduction Signs

Lane reduction signs warn drivers that a lane will end ahead.

  • Merge smoothly and early.
  • Check mirrors before moving.
  • Use turn signals properly.

🚶 Pedestrian-Only Roads

Authorized vehicles entering pedestrian-only roads must travel extremely slowly.

Pedestrian safety always takes priority in these areas.

🏍️ Motorcycle Prohibition Signs

Some roads prohibit motorcycles and ordinary motorized bicycles completely.

  • Restricted vehicles must not enter.
  • Follow posted signs carefully.
  • Violations may lead to penalties.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety

👥 Passenger Restrictions

New motorcycle riders cannot carry passengers until they gain enough riding experience.

  • Applies during the first year of licensing.
  • Passengers affect balance and braking.
  • Experience improves rider safety.

🧍 Proper Handlebar Grip

Riders should hold handlebars lightly with wrists angled naturally downward.

  • Improves steering control.
  • Reduces arm fatigue.
  • Maintains smoother riding posture.

⚠️ Motorcycle Riding Requires Skill

Motorcycle operation requires advanced awareness, balance, and control skills.

Riders are more exposed than automobile drivers and face greater injury risk.

🚦 Motorized Bicycle Speed Limits

Ordinary motorized bicycles generally must not exceed 30 km/h.

  • Applies even if road limits are higher.
  • Follow vehicle-specific regulations.
  • Speed control improves safety.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚸 Crosswalk Restrictions

Parking or stopping near pedestrian or bicycle crossings is prohibited.

  • Includes areas within 5 meters.
  • Protects pedestrian visibility.
  • Prevents hidden hazards.

🚌 Bus Stop Restrictions

Parking near bus stops may only be allowed outside operating hours if local rules permit.

  • Always check posted signs.
  • Do not block public transportation.
  • Maintain passenger safety.

🅿️ Parking Manual Vehicles

Manual transmission vehicles should always use the parking brake when parked.

Gear position alone is not enough for safe parking.

🚗 Proper Parking Position

Vehicles must park according to traffic direction and legal roadside conditions.

  • Improper parking may obstruct traffic.
  • Unsafe parking increases accident risk.
  • Always follow local parking regulations.

🚄 Railroad Crossing Safety

🚉 Never Block Railroad Crossings

Drivers must never enter a crossing if traffic congestion may trap the vehicle on the tracks.

  • Confirm traffic flow first.
  • Cross only when fully clear.
  • Blocking tracks is extremely dangerous.
Trains cannot stop quickly, so careful judgment is essential.

🚑 Emergency Situations

🚨 Emergency Vehicles

Drivers already inside an intersection should clear it safely before yielding to emergency vehicles.

  • Avoid blocking intersections.
  • Yield calmly and safely.
  • Watch surrounding traffic carefully.

🚧 After Traffic Accidents

After an accident, move the vehicle safely if possible and stop the engine.

  • Prevents secondary accidents.
  • Reduces traffic obstruction.
  • Improves roadside safety.

🛠️ Brake Failure on Slopes

If brakes fail downhill, roadside barriers or escape areas may help stop the vehicle safely.

Reducing impact safely is better than remaining uncontrolled.

🚨 Emergency Equipment

Many vehicles in Japan are required to carry emergency warning devices.

  • Flares
  • Smoke signals
  • Flashlights and warning tools

🚶 Pedestrians & Bicycle Safety

🚲 Hidden Cyclists Near Crossings

Drivers must use extreme caution near stopped vehicles close to bicycle crossings.

  • Pedestrians may be hidden.
  • Cyclists may suddenly appear.
  • Passing carelessly is dangerous.

🚶 Intersections Without Crosswalks

Drivers must still protect pedestrians even when no painted crosswalk exists.

  • Slow down near intersections.
  • Stop when necessary.
  • Do not interfere with pedestrian movement.

🏍️ Pushing a Motorcycle

A rider pushing a motorcycle with the engine off is generally treated as a pedestrian.

Sidewalk use may be allowed when done safely and carefully.

⚖️ Traffic Rules & Legal Responsibility

📋 Driver’s License Categories

Japan uses several license categories for different driving purposes.

  • First-class licenses
  • Second-class licenses
  • Learner’s permits

🔄 Turn Signal Timing

Lane changes should generally be signaled at least 3 seconds before movement.

  • Helps surrounding traffic react safely.
  • Improves predictability.
  • Reduces collision risk.

🚛 Cargo Safety Rules

Cargo must be loaded safely and within legal size limits.

  • Improper cargo may fall onto roads.
  • Unbalanced loads reduce stability.
  • Secure all items carefully.

🚓 Obeying Police & Controllers

Drivers must obey both traffic signals and authorized traffic controllers.

Police officer instructions take priority when directing traffic.

🚧 Overtaking Rules

Overtaking is generally performed from the right side in Japan.

  • Passing on the left is usually prohibited.
  • Always confirm safety first.
  • Use mirrors and signals carefully.

📌 Storage Location Sticker

Vehicles issued a storage location sticker must display it on the rear window.

  • Confirms legal parking registration.
  • Required under Japanese regulations.
  • Must remain visible.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

🚘 Smooth Expressway Driving

Drivers should avoid abrupt braking or sharp steering while traveling on expressways.

  • Maintain enough following distance.
  • Drive smoothly and predictably.
  • High-speed collisions are very dangerous.

🚚 Truck Speed Limits

Expressway speed limits may vary depending on signs, weather, and road conditions.

  • Always follow posted speed limits.
  • Reduce speed during poor weather.
  • Vehicle type restrictions may apply.

✅ Key Takeaways Checklist

🚘 Safe Driving

  • Slow down before curves
  • Use engine braking downhill
  • Take regular driving breaks

🌧️ Weather & Night Safety

  • Reduce speed at night
  • Watch for hydroplaning
  • Avoid headlight glare

🚦 Intersections

  • Yield properly at intersections
  • Watch blind spots carefully
  • Follow police directions

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety

  • Use proper riding posture
  • Follow passenger rules
  • Obey motorcycle speed limits

🚗 Parking Rules

  • Avoid parking near crossings
  • Use parking brakes properly
  • Follow bus stop restrictions

🚑 Emergency Preparedness

  • Carry emergency equipment
  • Move damaged vehicles safely
  • Yield to emergency vehicles
