🚶 Pedestrians & Road Sharing Safety

🚸 Pedestrians Near Intersections

Even without a marked crosswalk, drivers must still watch carefully for pedestrians and yield when necessary.

  • Reduce speed near intersections.
  • Watch for sudden crossing pedestrians.
  • Never assume vehicles always have priority.
Pedestrian safety is strongly protected under Japanese traffic rules.

🚲 Passing Bicycles Safely

Drivers should slow down and leave enough space when passing cyclists.

  • Bicycles may wobble suddenly.
  • Road obstacles may force movement.
  • Extra caution helps prevent accidents.

🚋 Tram & Streetcar Stops

Drivers must slow down near streetcar safety zones because passengers may enter the roadway unexpectedly.

  • Watch carefully for pedestrians.
  • Reduce speed near safety zones.
  • Stay alert around public transportation areas.

🧍 Pushing a Motorcycle

A person pushing a motorcycle with the engine off is generally treated similarly to a pedestrian.

Care should still be taken not to obstruct sidewalks or pedestrians.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety & Riding Skills

🌀 Riding Through Curves

Motorcycle riders should use smooth throttle control while cornering.

  • Slow down before entering curves.
  • Maintain stable throttle control.
  • Accelerate gently after exiting.
Sudden braking or acceleration during curves may reduce stability and traction.

🛡️ Protective Clothing & Helmets

Riders should wear approved helmets and protective clothing that covers most of the body.

  • Use PS(C) or JIS approved helmets.
  • Wear proper riding jackets and gloves.
  • Protect exposed skin even during hot weather.

👥 Carrying Passengers

Passengers may only ride on motorcycles properly equipped with passenger seats.

  • Ordinary mopeds cannot carry passengers.
  • Motorcycles must be designed for two riders.
  • Passenger safety equipment is important.

⚠️ Motorcycle Skill Awareness

Motorcycles require advanced balance, coordination, and hazard awareness.

Riders are more exposed to danger than automobile drivers.

🚦 Intersections & Traffic Flow

🛑 Unsignalized Intersections

Drivers should slow down and check carefully even if an intersection appears clear.

  • Watch for hidden vehicles.
  • Check for pedestrians and bicycles.
  • Never assume the road is completely safe.

🚑 Emergency Vehicles Near Intersections

Drivers should avoid blocking intersections when emergency vehicles approach.

Stop safely and yield properly according to surrounding traffic conditions.

🚧 Merging Traffic

Vehicles entering from merging lanes must adjust speed and merge safely into traffic.

  • Main-lane traffic usually has priority.
  • Use mirrors and blind spot checks.
  • Merge smoothly without forcing entry.

🌧️ Weather & Night Driving

🌙 High Beam Usage

Drivers should switch to low beams when following another vehicle closely.

  • Prevents glare in mirrors.
  • Protects visibility for other drivers.
  • Improves nighttime safety.

❄️ Snow & Ice Driving

Sudden braking or sharp steering on icy roads can easily cause skidding.

  • Drive slowly and smoothly.
  • Avoid abrupt steering movements.
  • Increase following distance.

💨 Strong Crosswinds

Tunnel exits and open roads may expose vehicles to dangerous side winds.

  • Reduce speed gradually.
  • Keep both hands firmly on the wheel.
  • Avoid sudden steering corrections.

💡 Headlight Glare Reduction

Looking slightly toward the left side of the road helps reduce glare from bright headlights.

Avoid staring directly into oncoming headlights at night.

🚧 Road Signs & Traffic Signs

🏫 School & Nursery Area Signs

These signs warn drivers that children and pedestrians may suddenly appear nearby.

  • Reduce speed immediately.
  • Watch carefully for crossing children.
  • Prepare to stop at any time.

🔔 Sound Horn Signs

Drivers are required to sound the horn in designated low-visibility areas.

  • Common near blind curves.
  • Often found on mountain roads.
  • Warns approaching vehicles and pedestrians.

↔️ Mandatory Direction Signs

Blue direction signs indicate the directions vehicles are required to follow.

These signs are instructions, not prohibitions.

🚌 Exclusive Bus Lanes

Bus lanes are reserved for designated vehicles and cannot be used simply because traffic is heavy.

  • Follow lane restrictions carefully.
  • Watch posted signs.
  • Do not enter unauthorized lanes.

🌬️ Strong Wind Warning Signs

These warning signs indicate areas where crosswinds may affect vehicle stability.

  • Reduce speed before entering.
  • Prepare for sudden side winds.
  • Maintain stable steering control.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚫 Sidewalk Parking

Vehicles should never block sidewalks or place tires onto pedestrian walking areas.

Sidewalks must remain safe and accessible for pedestrians.

🅿️ Parking Near Entrances

Parking within 3 meters of garages or parking entrances is prohibited.

  • Improves visibility.
  • Prevents traffic obstruction.
  • Keeps entrances usable.

🚒 Fire Hydrant Areas

Parking near fire hydrants and emergency equipment is prohibited.

  • Emergency access must remain clear.
  • Temporary stopping may be limited.
  • Always check local restrictions.

🚧 Construction Areas

Parking restrictions near roadwork zones help protect workers and traffic safety.

Construction areas often have reduced visibility and narrower road space.

⏱️ Waiting in No-Parking Areas

Remaining stopped while waiting for someone may still be treated as illegal parking.

  • Applies even if the driver stays inside.
  • Several minutes may still count as parking.
  • Always obey posted restrictions.

🚄 Railroad Crossing Safety

🚉 Signal-Controlled Railroad Crossings

Drivers may proceed according to traffic signals at certain railroad crossings.

  • Still confirm train safety carefully.
  • Watch barriers and warning lights.
  • Never rush through crossings.

🛑 Full Stops at Crossings

Most railway crossings require a complete stop even if visibility is clear.

Trains cannot stop quickly, so careful confirmation is essential.

🚘 General Driving Techniques

🌀 Handling Curves Properly

Drivers should slow down before entering curves and accelerate gradually after exiting.

  • Improves traction and balance.
  • Reduces skidding risk.
  • Maintains steering control.

🧍 Proper Driving Posture

A correct posture includes slightly bent knees and relaxed arms while driving.

  • Improves steering control.
  • Reduces fatigue.
  • Helps emergency reactions.

⛰️ Narrow Mountain Roads

Downhill vehicles usually yield to uphill vehicles on narrow mountain roads.

Restarting uphill is more difficult for ascending vehicles.

📏 Speed Awareness

Long periods of high-speed driving may distort a driver’s sense of speed.

  • Check the speedometer regularly.
  • Stay aware of legal limits.
  • Avoid unintentionally speeding.

🚑 Emergency Situations

🛑 Sudden Braking

Sudden braking should only be used during emergencies or unavoidable danger.

Abrupt braking without reason may create dangerous traffic situations.

🛠️ Disabled Vehicles on Expressways

Drivers should move disabled vehicles safely onto the shoulder whenever possible.

  • Avoid blocking traffic lanes.
  • Use hazard lights.
  • Prioritize safety first.

🔧 Vehicle Inspection & Maintenance

🚗 Vehicle Inspection Schedules

Private passenger vehicles are not normally inspected every six months.

  • Inspection schedules depend on vehicle type.
  • Follow legal inspection requirements.
  • Maintain vehicle condition regularly.

📋 Learner Driver Supervision

Learner drivers must practice under supervision from a qualified person.

Proper supervision improves driving safety and learning quality.

⚖️ Traffic Violations & Legal Responsibility

🚫 Suspended Licenses

Driving with a suspended license is a serious violation under Japanese law.

  • More severe than forgetting a license card.
  • May lead to heavy penalties.
  • Driving privileges are legally removed.

🚓 Illegal Parking Stickers

Illegal parking notices may only be removed by authorized persons related to the vehicle.

Unauthorized removal is prohibited.

🚛 Cargo Area Passengers

Only the minimum number of workers necessary for loading or unloading may ride in cargo areas.

  • Unnecessary passengers are prohibited.
  • Cargo areas are unsafe for passengers.
  • Follow legal passenger restrictions.

🚧 Overtaking Safety

Drivers should confirm safety before signaling and overtaking another vehicle.

  • Check mirrors and blind spots first.
  • Never force overtaking maneuvers.
  • Avoid illegal center-line crossing.
