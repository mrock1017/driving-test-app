🚧 Road Signs & Road Markings

🚲 Bicycle Road Markings

Some road markings regulate bicycle movement at intersections and on roadways.

  • Watch carefully for bicycle lanes and routes.
  • Do not block bicycle paths.
  • Understand the difference between bicycle crossings and bicycle lanes.
Bicycle-related signs and markings help reduce collisions between vehicles and cyclists.

🔔 Mandatory Horn Zones

Horn signs indicate areas where drivers must sound the horn because visibility is poor.

  • Often used on mountain roads.
  • Common near blind curves.
  • Helps warn approaching traffic.

🚫 Overtaking Prohibited Signs

These signs prohibit overtaking completely, even if the driver does not cross the center line.

Drivers must never pass another vehicle in overtaking-prohibited zones.

🚸 Safety Zone Signs

Safety zones are protected traffic areas designed for pedestrian safety and traffic organization.

  • Slow down near safety zones.
  • Do not park or block these areas.
  • Watch carefully for pedestrians.

🛣️ Speed Limit Signs

Road signs and pavement markings may both display the legal maximum speed limit.

  • Always obey posted limits.
  • Reduce speed during bad weather.
  • Adjust speed according to traffic conditions.

🚦 Intersections & Traffic Signals

➡️ Arrow Traffic Signals

Arrow signals only allow movement in the directions shown.

  • Right turns must wait for the right-turn arrow.
  • Do not move in prohibited directions.
  • Watch carefully for pedestrians.

🔴 Flashing Red Signals

Flashing red signals require drivers to stop completely before proceeding safely.

Treat flashing red lights similarly to stop signs.

👮 Police Officer Instructions

Police officer hand signals and traffic instructions take priority over normal traffic lights and signs.

  • Follow officer directions immediately.
  • Watch hand signals carefully.
  • Stay calm during congestion or emergencies.

🔄 U-Turns & Lane Changes

Before changing lanes, backing up, or making a U-turn:

  • Check mirrors and surroundings first.
  • Signal before moving.
  • Keep signaling until the maneuver is finished.

🏍️ Motorcycle Safety

🧍 Proper Riding Position

Riders should grip the fuel tank firmly with both knees and keep their feet pointed forward.

  • Improves balance and steering.
  • Reduces unnecessary body movement.
  • Helps maintain better control.

👥 Passenger Safety

Passengers may only ride on motorcycles designed and equipped for passenger transport.

Ordinary mopeds and unsuitable motorcycles should not carry passengers.

📦 Motorcycle Cargo Rules

Motorcycle cargo should be properly secured and must not extend excessively from the sides.

  • Prevents balance problems.
  • Reduces contact with obstacles.
  • Improves riding stability.

↪️ Two-Stage Right Turns

Some motorized bicycles are required to perform two-stage right turns at large intersections.

  • Approach safely near the center.
  • Follow road markings carefully.
  • Obey intersection signs.

🌧️ Weather & Night Driving

🌙 High Beam Usage

In busy city areas, drivers should normally use low beams instead of high beams.

  • High beams may blind others.
  • Pedestrians become harder to notice.
  • Excessive glare creates danger.

👁️ Dark Adaptation

Vision does not adjust immediately when moving from bright areas into darkness.

Drivers should slow down because temporary visibility becomes weaker.

🌧️ Hydroplaning

Hydroplaning occurs when water builds between the tires and the road surface.

  • Steering control becomes weaker.
  • Braking becomes difficult.
  • Reduce speed during rain.

💦 Slippery Roads After Rain Begins

Roads become especially slippery when rain first begins because oil and dust mix with water.

  • Brake gently.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Avoid sudden steering.

💡 Flashing Headlights

Drivers may briefly flash headlights near blind curves or low-visibility areas to warn others.

Excessive flashing may confuse other road users, so use it carefully.

🚶 Pedestrians & Bicycle Safety

🚲 Passing Bicycles Safely

Drivers should reduce speed and leave enough space when passing cyclists.

  • Cyclists may suddenly wobble.
  • Road obstacles may force movement.
  • Extra caution prevents accidents.

🚪 Crossing Sidewalks

Drivers entering or leaving parking lots or private property should stop and check sidewalks carefully.

Pedestrians or bicycles may suddenly appear from blind spots.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚫 Safety Zone Parking Restrictions

Parking near safety zones and within 10 meters of their edges is prohibited.

  • Protects pedestrian visibility.
  • Maintains traffic safety.
  • Prevents congestion.

🚫 Double Parking

Stopping beside another parked vehicle is dangerous and generally prohibited.

Double parking blocks traffic flow and increases collision risk.

🅿️ Parking Space Rules

Automobile owners must secure proper off-road parking close to the vehicle’s main place of use.

  • Helps reduce roadside parking.
  • Improves neighborhood safety.
  • Supports smoother traffic flow.

🚄 Railroad Crossing Safety

🚉 Signal-Controlled Crossings

Railroad crossings controlled by traffic signals may be crossed according to signal indications.

  • Still watch carefully for trains.
  • Check barriers and warnings.
  • Be prepared to stop immediately.

🚑 Emergency Situations

🚨 Emergency Vehicles

Drivers must safely move aside and yield when emergency vehicles approach.

  • On one-way roads, either side may be used if safer.
  • Do not block intersections.
  • Stay calm and cooperative.

🌏 Earthquake Situations

During major earthquakes, vehicles should remain accessible for emergency responders.

Avoid locking abandoned vehicles or blocking emergency routes.

🩹 Emergency Preparation

Responsible drivers should prepare for emergencies before accidents happen.

  • Maintain insurance coverage.
  • Learn basic first aid.
  • Carry emergency supplies.

🔧 Vehicle Inspection & Maintenance

💦 Washer Fluid Inspection

Drivers should check windshield washer fluid levels and spray direction regularly.

  • Maintains clear visibility.
  • Improves driving safety.
  • Helps during rain or dirt buildup.

🛑 Brake Inspection

If the brake pedal slowly sinks while pressed firmly, hydraulic brake problems may exist.

Brake fluid leaks or hydraulic failure should be inspected immediately.

🚘 General Driving Principles

⚙️ Engine Braking

Lower gears provide stronger engine braking effects than higher gears.

  • Useful on downhill roads.
  • Helps control speed safely.
  • Reduces brake overheating.

🌪️ Centrifugal Force

Higher speeds and tighter curves create stronger outward force during turns.

Drivers should reduce speed before entering curves.

↩️ Wheel Turning Paths

During turns, rear wheels follow a tighter path than front wheels.

  • Be careful near curbs.
  • Watch obstacles closely.
  • Allow enough turning space.

🤝 Cooperative Driving

Safe driving depends on patience, awareness, and consideration for others.

  • Avoid selfish behavior.
  • Respect surrounding traffic.
  • Drive calmly and responsibly.

😴 Driver Fatigue

Fatigue reduces concentration, steering ability, and braking reaction time.

Tired drivers are much more likely to cause accidents.

🚧 Construction Zones

Drivers should slow down and yield carefully near road construction areas.

  • Do not force entry into opposing lanes.
  • Watch for workers and equipment.
  • Proceed carefully and patiently.

⚖️ Accidents & Legal Responsibility

📞 Reporting Traffic Accidents

Even minor accidents must be reported to police.

  • Necessary for insurance claims.
  • Required for legal procedures.
  • Creates official accident records.
Private agreements alone are not enough under Japanese traffic law.
