🏍️ Motorcycle Safety & Riding Techniques

🛑 Proper Motorcycle Braking

Riders should use both the front and rear brakes together while keeping the motorcycle stable and balanced.

  • Keep handlebars straight while braking.
  • Avoid sudden leaning movements.
  • Brake smoothly to prevent skidding.
Balanced braking improves stopping control and reduces accident risk.

🧍 Stable Riding Posture

Motorcycle riders improve stability by gripping the fuel tank firmly with both knees.

  • Reduces unnecessary body movement.
  • Improves balance during turns.
  • Helps maintain smoother steering.

👥 Carrying Passengers

Passengers should only ride on motorcycles equipped with proper passenger seats.

  • Ordinary mopeds cannot carry passengers.
  • Single-seat motorcycles are not designed for double riding.
  • Proper seating improves safety and stability.

🚦 Intersections & Priority Rules

➡️ Completing Right Turns

If you legally entered an intersection on a green signal, complete the turn safely instead of stopping unnecessarily inside the intersection.

Stopping inside intersections may obstruct traffic and increase danger.

🛑 Intersections Without Stop Lines

When no stop line exists, stop where you can safely confirm traffic conditions.

  • Do not block pedestrians.
  • Maintain visibility.
  • Check both directions carefully.

🚗 Priority Roads

Vehicles traveling on the main road generally have priority over vehicles entering from smaller side roads.

  • Slow down before entering.
  • Yield when necessary.
  • Watch carefully for hidden traffic.

🛣️ Expressway Driving

🚘 Merging Safely

Drivers entering the expressway should carefully check vehicles already traveling in the main lane.

  • Use the acceleration lane properly.
  • Match traffic speed smoothly.
  • Check mirrors and blind spots.

⬇️ Leaving the Expressway

Slow down only after entering the deceleration lane.

Slowing down too early in the main lane may cause rear-end collisions.

🛑 Stopping Rules

Stopping or parking on expressways is generally prohibited except during emergencies.

  • Vehicle breakdowns
  • Traffic accidents
  • Police instructions

🌧️ Weather & Night Driving

🌧️ Rainy Weather Driving

Wet roads increase stopping distance and reduce tire grip.

  • Reduce speed.
  • Increase following distance.
  • Brake gently and early.

❄️ Snow & Ice Safety

Snow and icy roads are extremely slippery and dangerous.

  • Use winter tires or snow tires.
  • Install tire chains on driving wheels.
  • Avoid sudden braking or steering.

🌙 Night Driving

Drivers may feel slower at night even when driving fast.

  • Reduce speed at night.
  • Use low beams in traffic.
  • Avoid staring into headlights.
Looking slightly toward the left side of the road helps reduce glare.

🚗 Parking & Stopping Rules

🚌 Bus Stop Areas

Parking restrictions near bus stops depend on operating conditions and posted signs.

🚫 Double Parking

Stopping beside another parked vehicle is generally prohibited.

  • Blocks traffic flow
  • Reduces visibility
  • Creates hazards for cyclists

🚶 Crosswalk Safety

Parking or stopping near pedestrian crossings and bicycle crossings is prohibited.

Vehicles near crossings block visibility and increase accident risk.

🚸 Pedestrians & Cyclist Safety

🚲 Bicycle Crossing Zones

Drivers must allow bicyclists to cross safely at bicycle crossing areas.

  • Stop smoothly when necessary.
  • Avoid aggressive driving.
  • Watch carefully for sudden movement.

👧 Children Crossing Areas

Areas near schools and parks require extra caution because children may suddenly enter the roadway.

  • Reduce speed.
  • Prepare to stop quickly.
  • Stay alert at all times.

🚧 Road Signs & Traffic Rules

⚠️ Lane Reduction Sign

This warning sign indicates that traffic lanes will decrease ahead.

  • Merge carefully.
  • Check mirrors early.
  • Maintain safe spacing.

🔔 Mandatory Horn Zones

Some mountain roads and blind curves require drivers to sound the horn.

Horn usage helps warn hidden vehicles and pedestrians.

🚫 Vehicle Restriction Signs

Certain roads prohibit small vehicles, mopeds, or low-engine motorcycles.

  • Always obey posted restrictions.
  • Confirm your vehicle category.
  • Follow lane instructions carefully.

🛡️ Safe Driving Habits

🚘 Safe Following Distance

Vehicles cannot stop instantly during emergencies.

  • Maintain enough space ahead.
  • Avoid tailgating.
  • Adjust speed according to traffic.

⚙️ Engine Braking

Lower gears provide stronger engine braking effects.

  • Useful on downhill roads.
  • Helps control speed safely.
  • Reduces brake wear.

📦 Cargo Safety

Cargo should be kept low and distributed evenly.

Uneven cargo increases rollover risk and reduces stability.

🚑 Emergency Situations

🩹 Helping Injured People

Provide first aid carefully and avoid moving people with possible head or neck injuries unless absolutely necessary.

🌏 Earthquake While Driving

During a major earthquake:

  • Avoid sudden braking.
  • Stop safely after checking surroundings.
  • Keep roads clear for emergency vehicles.
