Honmen Master is designed to simulate the structure of the Japanese full driving license written exam.

{{ ui.how_it_works }}

  • Questions 1–90 are standard True/False questions.
  • Questions 91–95 are group questions with illustrations.
  • Each group question is worth 2 points.
  • If one answer inside a group question is wrong, that group question becomes 0 points.
  • The total score is 100 points.
  • The passing target is 90 out of 100.
  • Every attempt is randomly generated from the {{ ui.honmen_question_bank }}.
Pay special attention to the final group questions. They carry higher value and test hazard prediction, road signs, and real driving judgment.
